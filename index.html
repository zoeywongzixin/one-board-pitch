Today, announcements are spread across WhatsApp, Spectrum, email and Instagram, and each student is in more than ten groups. Important messages get buried, so students walk to cancelled classes or work to outdated deadlines. Existing platforms store announcements but never update your timetable. Lecturers post the same notice several times and still get asked 'Is class on today?'. And old messages are almost impossible to search.

Objectives

  1. 01One platform for all course announcements
  2. 02Auto-update timetables
  3. 03Auto-sync deadlines
  4. 04Notify only when it affects you
  5. 05Post once, reach every student
  6. 06Searchable announcement archive
We have six objectives. First, one platform for all course and OCC announcements. Second, timetables that update automatically when classes change. Third, deadlines that sync to students' calendars. Fourth, notifications only when a change affects you. Fifth, lecturers post once and reach every affected student. And sixth, a searchable archive of past announcements.

One Board has three users. Students view their live timetable, deadline calendar and searchable announcements. Lecturers post announcements, change classes and set deadlines. Admin staff manage courses and users and post faculty-wide notices. It's a web app for FCSIT only. Integration with Spectrum, WhatsApp and UM email is out of scope.
